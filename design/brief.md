# Via asset brief: PawlyPaint

Soft, cozy kawaii style matching the redesign art in `assets/art/` (Mochi, tier frames, icons): rounded shapes, gentle shading, thick soft outlines, no harsh black.
Palette: rose pink `#E5739F` (theme seed), blush `#FCE4EC`, paper white `#FFFBF7`, warm brown `#8D5A3B`, deep cocoa `#3B2A2F`.
Mood: a keepsake photo for a child's finished jewel painting. Style references: `assets/art/tiers/gold.png`, `assets/art/icons/gem_paw.png`.

## Puzzle pictures (PAP-71)
Pixel art for the paint/sort pictures: each picture is painted gem by gem on a square grid, 16x16 (Easy), 24x24 (Medium), 32x32 (Hard) or 40x40 (Extreme). Draw it as a pixel art sprite on exactly that grid: every pixel a big, equal, sharp square block, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no dithering noise, no text. Cute, cozy kawaii look with a 1-pixel darker outline on the subject, one plain background color, and a limited palette: Easy 4-6 colors, Medium 6-8, Hard up to 12, Extreme up to 16. Small details (shine, eyes, cheeks) are single deliberate pixels.
